        Tips for Bluetooth headphones and AirPods
        • Pair and connect them before you open this page, or click Refresh devices after they connect.
        • Choose them as the Speaker to hear your playback in them.
        • Using the headset as the Microphone works too, but many headsets switch to lower "call quality" sound while their mic is on. Your laptop's mic often sounds better on the recording.
        • If a device doesn't show up, check that it's selected in your computer's sound settings, then refresh.
